## Unit 093. He speaks English very well. (word order 1)

### A

The *verb* and the *object* are usually together.
- Sue reads a newspaper every day.
- He speaks English very well.
- I like Italian food very much.
- Did you watch television all evening?
- Paul often wears a black hat.
- We invited a lot of people to the party.
- I opened the door slowly.
- Why do you always make the same mistake?
- I'm going to borrow some money from the bank.

### B

Place (*where*?) is usually before time (*when*?).
- We went to the party last night.
- Lisa walks to work every day.
- Will you be at home this evening?
- I usually go to bed early.
- We arrived at the airport at 7 o'clock.
- They've lived in the same house for 20 years.
- Joe's father has been in hospital since June.
